# Third party components

The exact trained checkpoint was supplied by the project owner in train-2.zip. Model dataset/weight rights remain those of their original sources.

The bundled MiniLM cache is Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2, an ONNX conversion of sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2. Model card and license metadata: https://huggingface.co/Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 . Runtime dependencies retain their own licenses.

Ultralytics documentation and licensing: https://docs.ultralytics.com/usage/python/ and https://www.ultralytics.com/license . PDFKit and frontend/npm dependencies retain their packaged license files after installation. Embedded report fonts include their license in backend/reports/assets.

Gemini generateContent interface: https://ai.google.dev/api/generate-content . Provider access and billing belong to the operator's own account.
